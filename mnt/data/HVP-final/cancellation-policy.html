Cancellation Policy

Cancellation conditions depend on how your reservation was made and the terms attached to that reservation.

Direct reservations

For a direct reservation, please contact Hotel Vishaka Paark with your booking details. The hotel team will explain the cancellation conditions applicable to your reservation.

OTA reservations

Reservations made through MakeMyTrip, Booking.com, Agoda, Cleartrip or another third-party platform are subject to the cancellation terms shown with the relevant reservation.

Changes and refunds

Any applicable cancellation charges, deadlines or refund conditions depend on the booking terms attached to the reservation.

For further details, please contact Hotel Vishaka Paark directly.

This page is a general guide and does not replace the cancellation terms shown on a confirmed reservation. The applicable booking confirmation or OTA terms will prevail.
