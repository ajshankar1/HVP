Privacy Policy

Hotel Vishaka Paark respects the privacy of guests and website visitors. This notice explains, at a general level, how information may be handled when you use this website.

1. Information you provide

If you submit an enquiry or booking request, the website may receive information such as your name, phone number, email address, stay dates, room preference, guest details and any message or special request you choose to provide.

2. How information may be used

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, assist with reservation requests, communicate about a stay, provide requested hotel information and support guest service.

3. Booking and payment platforms

If you choose to book through MakeMyTrip, Booking.com, Agoda, Cleartrip or another third-party service, information submitted through that service is handled according to the service's own privacy notice and terms.

4. WhatsApp, email and telephone

If you choose to contact the hotel through WhatsApp, email or telephone, information shared through that channel may be processed by the relevant communication provider as well as by the hotel for the purpose of responding to you.

5. Weather information

The website's live Coimbatore weather feature uses a third-party weather data service. Weather requests may be sent to that service to retrieve current and forecast information. The weather feature is not used as a hotel booking or identity-verification service.

6. Cookies and technical information

The website may use normal browser storage, technical data or similar mechanisms required for website functionality. If analytics, advertising or additional tracking services are added later, this policy should be updated to describe them accurately.

7. Data security

Reasonable measures should be used to protect information handled through the website and hotel enquiry process. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data retention and requests

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, operational records, legal obligations or dispute resolution. For questions about information associated with an enquiry or booking, please contact the hotel directly.

9. Third-party websites

Links to OTAs, Tripadvisor, social media and other websites are provided for convenience. Their privacy practices are controlled by those third parties, not by this website.

10. Updates to this policy

This privacy notice may be updated when the website's data collection, booking workflow or third-party services change. The latest published version should be treated as the current website notice.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact Hotel Vishaka Paark at 0422 4695952, +91 90909 27979 or hotelvishakapaark@gmail.com.

Important: This is a general website privacy notice. Before the website goes fully live, the hotel should review this policy against its actual hosting, forms, booking workflow, analytics, messaging systems and any data processors used by the hotel.
