A city of experiences.

Discover temples, museums, parks, shopping destinations and scenic escapes around Coimbatore.

Marudhamalai Temple

A well-known hill temple dedicated to Lord Murugan and one of Coimbatore's prominent spiritual destinations.

Perur Pateeswarar Temple

A historic temple dedicated to Lord Shiva, known for its traditional architecture and cultural significance.

G.D. Naidu Museum

A destination for visitors interested in science, engineering, innovation and the legacy of G.D. Naidu.

V.O.C. Park & Zoo

A family-oriented city attraction with green spaces and recreational surroundings.

Singanallur Lake

A prominent urban lake offering a quieter side of Coimbatore and an opportunity to experience the city's natural surroundings.

Brookefields Mall

A popular shopping and entertainment destination in Coimbatore.

Valparai

A scenic hill destination in the Western Ghats, known for tea estates, forests and mountain landscapes.
